STORYBOARD · THE ROUTE IN DETAIL

Five stops from first answer to first follow-up.

The long version of block 6 on the Home page, like MYF's five stops. Each stop says what you do and what WebGen does. On the Home page it shrinks to three: Check, Route, Launch.

1

Brief

Briefing
YOU DO
Answer a few short questions about your business, your customers and your tone.
WEBGEN DOES
Turns your answers into a clear message and a plan for the site.
2

Draft

Eerste versie
YOU DO
Read the first version and see your business on screen.
WEBGEN DOES
Writes the copy, builds the sections and sets up the forms.
3

Edit

Aanpassen
YOU DO
Click any text to change it, or ask for a change in plain words.
WEBGEN DOES
Applies the change, keeps the design consistent, and can translate a page into up to eight languages.
4

Launch

Live zetten
YOU DO
Connect your own domain and press publish.
WEBGEN DOES
Checks the setup, serves the site securely and keeps checking that it stays set up.
5

Follow up

Opvolgen
YOU DO
Choose who gets which email and when.
WEBGEN DOES
Sends from your own address with consent and unsubscribe handled, and shows the results.
