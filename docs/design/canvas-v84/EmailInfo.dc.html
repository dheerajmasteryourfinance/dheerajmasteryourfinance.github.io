Who sent it

WebGen is a website and email platform. Businesses use it to run their website and to email the people who signed up there. The email came from that business, not from WebGen itself. Their name and address are in the email's footer.

Why it reached you

Most likely you filled in a form on that business's website, such as a newsletter sign-up, a contact form or a free download. If the business asks new subscribers to confirm, you would also have clicked a confirmation link first.
