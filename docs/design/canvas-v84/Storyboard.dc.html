STORYBOARD · DRAFT FOR REVIEW

WebGen: the Launch Route.

The same story method as MYB and MYF. MYB flies a Business Flight Plan, MYF follows a Financial GPS. WebGen follows a Launch Route: a route on a map with stops, from your first answer to your first follow-up email.

TAKEN FROM MYB (READ FROM THE LIVE SITE)

A promise with three checkmarks. "Does this sound familiar?" list. Three steps named Check, Route, Plan. A free low-commitment entry (the Flight Check). A "this is your business, 90 days from now" block and an "and if nothing changes?" block.

TAKEN FROM MYF (READ FROM THE LIVE SITE)

One travel object used throughout (Financial GPS). Numbered stops with "You do / We do" under each. A before and after block. Industries, FAQ and a free health check before the call.

WHAT WE DO DIFFERENTLY

WebGen is a tool, not a service, so the route is the product itself: brief, draft, edit, launch, follow up. The story stays the same, the promise is "Get found. Get signed up. Follow up."
