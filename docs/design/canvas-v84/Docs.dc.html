Build your first website

Go from a blank account to a published site. You answer a few questions about your business, WebGen builds the site, and you polish it in the editor.

About 10 minutes·Updated [DATE]
Before you start: have a short description of your business, your ideal customer and a few photos ready. You can skip the photos and add them later.

1. Start a new website

Sign in and click New website on your dashboard.

2. Answer the brief

The brief asks about your business the way a copywriter would. The answers become the words on your site, so write them as you would explain to a customer.

  • Your business name and your ideal customer
  • What your customer wants, and what stands in their way
  • Why customers can trust you, and the main action on your site
  • Your plan in three steps, your languages and, if you like, a preferred style
Tip: questions marked optional can be left blank. Short, honest answers work better than long ones.

3. Add photos

Upload your own photos or describe the ones you want. WebGen places them on the right pages.

[SCREENSHOT: Add photos step]

4. Review and edit

Your site opens in the editor. From here you can:

  • Click any text to change it
  • Adjust colours and fonts under Design
  • Ask the AI assistant for bigger changes, such as "add a pricing section"
  • Go back to an earlier version under History

Click Save when you are happy with your changes.

5. Publish

Click Publish. Your site goes online at a free address, and you can add your own domain afterwards.

Note: publishing needs an active subscription. [CONFIRM wording against billing rules]
