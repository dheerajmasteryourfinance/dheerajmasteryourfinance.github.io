1 · The idea

Every business has a story. Webgnr tells it.

Webgnr turns a few honest answers into your brand story, a website that tells it, and the forms and email to follow up.

For owners of small businesses who have no designer, no developer and no time to learn five tools. The product asks about customers, not about code.

The three words

Get found.A complete site, written from your brief, in your language, on your own domain.
Get signed up.Forms on every page, one audience, consent and unsubscribe handled.
Follow up.Newsletters and automatic emails from your own domain.

Dutch: Word gevonden. Krijg aanmeldingen. Volg op. Always in this order, always three, always full stops. They are the spine of the site, the video and every campaign.

1 · Your brand story, written for you

Answer a few questions in your own words. Webgnr turns them into your story and builds the site around it.

2 · AI that designs and writes

Change the site, translate it and draft campaigns by asking in plain words.

3 · Start free, grow at fair rates

Try it free for 14 days. Choose a plan when you are ready. No price talk until the plans are final.

These are the three advantages Joel asked for, in short form. Use them in this order wherever space allows.

2 · Voice and tone

Plain, warm, specific, honest.

Plain

A baker understands every sentence. Short words, short sentences, one idea at a time.

Specific

Name the thing: "Sourdough, baked fresh every morning", not "quality products".

Warm and direct

Talk to one person: you. In Dutch use je and jij, never u.

Honest

No invented numbers, ratings or quotes. Only claim what the product does today. Unconfirmed means unsaid.

5 · Type

Two typefaces, both free.

Aa

Bricolage Grotesque

Headlines, numbers, button words and the logo. ExtraBold (800) for display, Bold (700) for sub-heads. Tracking -3.5% at large sizes, line-height 1.05–1.15. Free on Google Fonts.

Aa

DM Sans

Everything you read: body, captions, forms, buttons. Regular (400), Medium (500), Semibold (600), Bold (700). Line-height 1.6. Free on Google Fonts.

The route

A dotted line with numbered stops: the journey from brief to launch. Use it to connect steps, never as decoration alone.

Shape: 20–24 px radius on cards, full pills on buttons, 1 px lines (#E3E8F0), generous space, no heavy shadows. Icons: a plain tick or arrow, 2.4 px stroke, round ends. Photos: real, warm and local; no stock handshakes.

7 · Motion and video

Quick to start, soft to land.

Easing and timing

Entrances and scroll revealscubic-bezier(.2, .7, .2, 1) · 0.3–0.7 s · rise 22–28 px, fade inBounce, for a thing that landscubic-bezier(.3, 1.4, .5, 1) · 0.6 sExitscubic-bezier(.7, 0, .2, 1) · 0.45 s · up 3% and fade

Rules

  • Move things with opacity and position, never with blur on large areas.
  • Text appears word by word; the weight grows from 300 to 800 as it lands.
  • Scenes change with hard cuts, not fades.
  • Everything stops for people who ask for reduced motion.
  • One moment of delight per screen, not five.

Email rules

  • Width 600 px, white card on surface, 16 px radius.
  • Header navy band with the mark and word. No banner images.
  • Headline Bricolage 800, 30 px. Greet with {{first_name}} when known.
  • Body DM Sans 16 px / 1.6, navy-slate text. One idea per email.
  • Button one, aqua with navy text, pill. Links in text use blue, never aqua.
  • Footer company line in muted 12 px. The app adds the sender details and the unsubscribe link to every email.
  • Subject under 45 characters, plain, benefit first: "Your site is ready to review". No capitals shouting, no emoji strings.
  • Preheader 60–90 characters that finish the subject.
  • Sender a person at Webgnr, for example "Anna at Webgnr", from webgnr.com.
