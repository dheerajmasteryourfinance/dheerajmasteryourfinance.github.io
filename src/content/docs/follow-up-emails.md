<div class="callout"><strong>Before you start:</strong> you need a form on your site that asks permission for follow-up emails. Without that checkbox, nobody can join a series. See <a href="/docs/forms-and-leads/">Forms and leads</a>.</div>

A follow-up series is a set of emails that go out one after another to visitors who fill in a form and tick **Yes, you may send me follow-up emails**. Each form can have its own series.

## 1. Turn on permission for your form

In the editor, select the section with your contact, download or newsletter form, and turn on **Ask permission for follow-up emails**. For a popup, select the section with its button and do the same. If you skip this, the **Follow-up emails** tab warns you that nobody will receive the emails.

## 2. Open Follow-up emails

Click **Manage**, then the **Follow-up emails** tab. Your forms are listed there. If your site has no forms yet, add a contact, download or newsletter form in the editor first.

## 3. Set up a series

Next to your form, click **Set up follow-up emails**, then **Open**. Give the series a **Name (only you see this)**, such as "Welcome series".

## 4. Add your first email

Click **Add email**, then fill in:

- **Subject**
- **Message**
- **Send after the form is sent**: a whole number and a **Unit** of minutes, hours or days, up to 365 days. The first email can also go out right after the form is sent.

Add more emails the same way. For each one after the first, the timing reads **Send after the previous email**. A series can have up to 20 emails, and each shows how long after the form or the previous email it goes out.

## 5. Preview and test

Click **Preview** to see how an email looks. The unsubscribe link only works in real emails. Click **Send me a test** to receive a copy yourself. Use **Edit** to change an email later, or delete one you no longer want.

## 6. Choose the sender

Under **Send as**, pick the address the emails come from. It shows the site's sending address, or Webgnr's own address until you set one up. Replies go to you. See [Set up your sender](/docs/set-up-your-sender/).

## 7. Turn it on

A series needs at least one email before it can be turned on. Click **Turn on** and the status changes to **On**. From then on, everyone who submits the form and ticks the box joins the series.

You can see how it is going on the same page, for example how many people are in progress, finished or stopped (unsubscribed or undeliverable).

## Pause or stop

Click **Pause** to hold the series. Nothing is lost: emails that are due wait and go out when you turn it back on. People already in the series keep receiving their emails unless you pause or delete it. **Delete follow-up emails** removes the series and its history, but your form submissions are kept.

<div class="callout callout-note"><strong>Note:</strong> every follow-up email includes an unsubscribe link, and anyone who unsubscribes stops getting them. Your business name and postal address from <strong>Campaigns</strong> appear in the footer.</div>
